Privacy Policy

AaaS (“we”) runs an agents-as-a-service workspace for the business that creates an account. This notice says what we collect, why, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Who it applies to

The account owner, their staff, and the people whose names, phone numbers or emails the owner stores in the CRM (clients, leads and similar). Industry is chosen at signup. A dental clinic and a digital marketing agency use the same product with different labels.

What we store

  • Account data: name, email, password hash, the industry you picked, and your plan status.
  • CRM data you type or import: contacts, companies, deals, notes, tasks, and files you upload. This stays in AaaS. We do not sync it to Salesforce or any other external CRM.
  • Drafts and approvals. A message is not sent until you approve that draft.
  • Your AI key, encrypted. We store a hint of the last four characters. Prompts go to the provider you chose. You pay that provider.
  • A WhatsApp QR session, encrypted, only if you link a number. The official WhatsApp API is optional and separate.

Why

To run the workspace you asked for: enquiries, follow-ups, content drafts, reports and invoices. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your customer records to train a model.

Consent for patient or client messaging

Before an approved WhatsApp or email goes to a person, you confirm they were told you may message them about the enquiry, appointment, campaign or invoice they asked about, and that they can say STOP. Opt-out blocks later sends. Messaging a clinic patient is limited to enquiries and appointments, not diagnosis.

How long

We keep account and CRM records while the account is open, and for up to 30 days after you delete them, unless a law tells us to keep a record longer. Server logs that can identify a person are kept for up to 90 days.

Deletion

In the app, open Privacy and submit a deletion request, or use the deletion page. You can also delete the account with your password. We will confirm to the email on the account.
