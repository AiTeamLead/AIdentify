Questions

Before you get started.

What is AIdentify?

A workspace to organise customers, follow-ups and invoices, with AI help for everyday tasks.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Create an account and choose your industry. To use AI, connect a supported provider with your own API key. Provider charges may apply.

Will AI send messages for me?

No. You review and approve drafts first. Sending also requires a connected channel.

Can I use it for my business?

AIdentify has workspaces for clinics, coaches, real estate, salons, home services, institutes and more. Clinic tools are for administration, not medical records or advice.

Can I manage customers and invoices?

Yes. Keep customer records, enquiries, follow-ups and invoices together. You can also import or export CSV files.

Does it sync with Salesforce or HubSpot?

No. Your records stay in AIdentify and don’t automatically sync with those services.

Is it free to sign up?

There’s no credit card required to create an account. AI use needs your own provider key, and provider charges may apply.

Can I send messages on WhatsApp?

Connect and configure a supported channel first. You approve every AI-written message before sending.

How is my data handled?

See our privacy and data deletion information for details.
