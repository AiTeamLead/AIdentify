The problem

The follow-up waits until someone is free.

A new enquiry, a quiet client and an unpaid invoice all sit in the same chat. The person who would answer is already on a call. The work is not hard. It just does not get drafted.

Sales slips

A WhatsApp or a website form arrives. Nobody writes the first reply while the owner is with another client.

Follow-ups go cold

The lead or the client goes quiet. The nudge is still in someone’s head, not in a draft.

Billing waits

The invoice and the reminder are a task for later. Later keeps moving.

The benefit

The draft is ready. The send is still yours.

AaaS reads this account’s records and writes the next note. Approving is what lets it go out. Rejecting skips it. The product does not post, email or message on its own.

Chief answers from the records

Features

What is actually on the screen.

Chief and the six agents

Chief is the chat on this account. On the clinic pack the agents are Front Desk, Patient Recall, Reviews & Referrals, Social Media, Owner Insights and Billing & Invoicing. Other packs rename them. They draft. You decide.

A CRM that stays here

Contacts, enquiries and visits, with tags and a timeline. Export CSV when you want a copy. There is no Salesforce, HubSpot or other CRM sync.

Nothing leaves without approval

The inbox holds every outbound draft. Approve records your OK. Reject skips it. With no WhatsApp provider connected, the button keeps a click-to-chat link and the screen says AaaS does not send it.

WhatsApp for the business is a QR you scan on a server that stays running. Inbound can land in the CRM. Outbound still needs approval. Bulk and cold blasts are blocked. The Cloud API is an optional upgrade, not the default.

Trust and privacy

Your key. Your records. A lawyer still has to read the pages.

Your own AI key

The key is checked once and stored encrypted. The screen shows the last four characters, not the key. Agent calls for that account use that key. They do not fall back to a shared key.

Data stays in this system

Customer records are not synced out to an external CRM. You can export CSV. Account deletion removes that account’s rows. The privacy, terms and deletion pages say they need a lawyer’s review. They are not a compliance certificate.

DPDP, written as a starting point

The pages name a grievance contact and a deletion request. Consent for messaging a client is the owner’s duty. This site does not claim the product is certified under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

Questions

What you need before the first draft.

What do I need to start?

An email, a password, the industry, the business name and city, and your own AI key from OpenRouter, OpenAI, xAI, Gemini, Anthropic or Amazon Bedrock. Creating the account does not charge a card. There is no shared AI allowance for a new account.

Will it message my customers by itself?

No. Drafts wait in Approvals. Approve records your OK. Reject skips the draft. A payment reminder is the same: queued, not sent, until you approve it.

Where do customer records go?

They stay in the AaaS CRM on this account. The product does not sync them to Salesforce, HubSpot or another CRM. CSV export is the copy you can take with you.

How does WhatsApp work?

You scan a QR from a worker that stays running. Inbound messages can become CRM contacts. Outbound still needs approval, one person at a time. Bulk sends are blocked. An official Cloud API is optional and uses keys you provide.

Is this legal advice about DPDP?

No. Privacy, terms and the deletion request are drafts for a lawyer to review. They name a grievance contact. They are not a certificate.

Can I change industry later?

An account that has picked an industry keeps it. The sample business can switch, so a demo can show every pack. A second business is a new account.
