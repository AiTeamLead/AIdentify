Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AIdentify?

AIdentify brings AI agents, customer records, follow-ups, invoices and approvals together in one workspace.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Create an account and choose your industry. To use AI agents, connect a supported provider with your own API key.

Do I need my own AI API key?

You can start on a $1 platform credit. After that, connect your own key or choose the self-hosted or hosted plan. Your provider bills usage on your key.

Will AI send messages automatically?

No. Outgoing drafts wait for your review and approval. Sending also requires a connected channel.

Can I use AIdentify for my industry?

Industry packs are available for clinics, coaches and consultants, real estate, salons and studios, home services, coaching institutes and other businesses. Clinic tools are for administration, not medical records or advice.

Can I manage all my customers in one place?

Yes. Organise customer records, enquiries, follow-ups and invoices in your AIdentify workspace. CSV export is available.

Does AIdentify connect with Salesforce or HubSpot?

Customer records stay in AIdentify and are not automatically synced with Salesforce, HubSpot or another external CRM.

Is there a free account?

You can create an account without a credit card. AI usage requires your own provider key, and your provider may charge for usage.
