# AIdentify user guide and workflows (v2.10)

Every business gets the same screens. Only the words (who you serve, what a booking is called), the suggestions and
the checks follow your industry. The same guide is inside the app: **Guide** in the sidebar, written with your own
business name and services.

## 1. The screens

| Sidebar | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Revenue, payments due, today's schedule, open enquiries, follow-ups due, this week's summary. |
| **Front Desk** | **+ New enquiry** and the booking button; tabs: Enquiries · Bookings · Follow-ups · Reviews & Referrals. WhatsApp messages arrive here. |
| **CRM** | Every customer and lead with phone, bookings, invoices and what they owe. Search by name, phone or email. |
| **Social Media** | Festival calendar and post drafts. Tick Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X or Select all. |
| **Billing & Invoicing** | **+ New invoice**, payment link, Mark paid, Queue reminder, PDF. |
| **Approvals** | Nothing is sent or posted until you approve it here. |
| **Skills** | Instructions your AI team follows in every draft ("Always mention free parking"). Up to 12. |
| **Guide** | This workflow, in your business's words. |
| **Settings** | Business profile · AI & API key · Integrations · Usage · Privacy & data. |

Chief (bottom right) is the chat assistant. Bot Mode (top right) opens the full workplace.

## 2. Setting up (about 2 minutes)

1. **Create account**, then **sign in** with the same email and password. You stay signed in on that browser.
2. **Business**: pick your industry tile, press OK.
3. **Name**: letters only, 2–60 characters. Numbers and symbols are not accepted.
4. **Locations**: search and pick your cities. Not in the list? Type the place and choose **Other**. Tap a city again to remove it.
5. **Services & offers**: tap suggestions or type your own. Typed entries must be real words and must fit your industry (a coaching institute cannot add "Facial").
6. **Customers**: tap who you want to reach, or type your own.
7. **AI key**: paste your key and press OK (it is tested), or Skip and add it later in Settings.
8. **Review**, then **Create my team**. Your account starts empty: no sample data.

Then: Settings › Integrations › **Connect with QR (free)** for WhatsApp, and connect Gmail and your social pages.

## 3. The workflow for each business

The seven steps are the same everywhere: **Enquiry → Booked → Completed → Invoice → Paid → Review & referral → Follow-up.**

### Real estate (`/Realestate`)

The left menu is the same as every other business. Front Desk opens on the **Pipeline**: one row per lead with what they want, the score, the stage and one button for the next step.

```
Enquiry (website, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, call, walk-in)
  -> Same phone number already a lead?  yes: update that lead   no: new lead
  -> Collect requirements: budget, location, BHK, size, when they want to buy
       details missing?  -> message asking for them (waits in Approvals)
  -> Search the Catalogue: available, price up to 10% over budget, right area, right BHK
       nothing fits?     -> says which wish blocks it, drafts "shall I widen the search?"
  -> Rank matches (inside budget first, closest to budget)
  -> Lead score 0-100 -> Hot (70+) act today | Warm (40-69) every 3 days | Cold monthly
  -> Send shortlist    WhatsApp only if the buyer agreed; otherwise call or email
  -> Interested?       no: record why, plan next follow-up
                       no answer 3 times: monthly nurturing
  -> Book site visit   slot taken? offers free times
                       booked: confirmation + note to the sales representative
                       day before: reminder
  -> Visit             did not come: offers a new time
                       came, not ready: follow-up
                       came, ready: handed to the sales team with a full brief
  -> Negotiation and documents (your people)
  -> Sale confirmed?   yes: Closed Won, property marked Sold,
                            booking-amount invoice -> payment -> review -> referral
                       no: back to follow-up
  -> At any point: Mark as Lost (with reason) or "Customer said stop" (no more messages)
  -> Dashboard: hot / warm / cold, follow-ups due, funnel, conversion, lost reasons
```

| Step | Where | What you press |
|---|---|---|
| Add your properties | Settings › Catalogue | **Add property**, or paste a list (Name, Area, BHK, Price, Size, Status) |
| New lead | Front Desk | **+ New enquiry**, or **Accept** on a WhatsApp message |
| Requirements | Pipeline › open the lead | Fill the form, **Save and find properties** |
| Shortlist | Lead window | **Send shortlist** |
| Their answer | Lead window | **Yes, interested** / **Not interested** / **No answer yet** |
| Site visit | Lead window | Pick property and time, **Check the calendar and book** |
| After the visit | Lead window | **Visited, ready to proceed** / **Visited, not ready yet** / **Did not come** |
| Sale | Lead window | **Sale confirmed: Closed Won**, then **Create booking-amount invoice** |
| Closing a lead | Lead window › Close this lead | **Mark as Lost** or **Customer said stop** |

How the score is built: properties fit the budget +30, best match inside budget +10, buying now +30 (1–3 months +20, 3–6 months +10), all details given +10, agreed to WhatsApp +5, said interested +15, came by referral or walk-in +10, each unanswered follow-up −10.

Ask Chief: "hot leads", "site visits", "leads due", "pipeline".

## 3a. The lead workflow in every industry (v2.13)

Every industry now runs the same lead flow as real estate, from its own flowchart and in its own words. Front Desk opens on the **Pipeline**; add what you sell in **Settings › Catalogue** first.

```
Enquiry -> existing customer? (update, no duplicate) -> collect requirements -> missing? ask
  -> search catalogue -> no match? explain and ask to adjust -> rank -> score -> Hot / Warm / Cold
  -> send details (WhatsApp only with consent) -> interested? -> book the meeting (slot check, alternatives)
  -> notify the right person -> meeting -> feedback -> ready? -> handoff -> confirmed? -> won
  -> invoice -> payment -> review -> referral        (Lost with reason / "customer said stop" at any point)
```

| Industry | Channels | Requirements asked | Catalogue holds | Meeting | Notified | Handoff | Won stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Digital marketing agency | Website, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Meta Ads | Goals, budget, channels, timeline | Packages | Discovery call, then strategy call | Account manager | Sales team: negotiation and contract | Closed Won |
| Dental clinic | Call, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google | Treatment needed, pain, budget, urgency | Treatments and doctors | Appointment / consultation | Front desk | Doctor: treatment plan, cost estimate, consent | Treatment Started |
| Skin, hair and aesthetic clinic | Instagram, WhatsApp, Website, Walk-in | Concern and goal, budget, downtime | Treatments and specialists | Consultation and skin analysis | Clinic coordinator | Specialist: package proposal and pricing | Closed Won |
| Coach or consultant | LinkedIn, Instagram, Website, Referral | Goal, challenge, timeline, budget | Programs | Discovery call | Coach | Coach: proposal and agreement | Closed Won |
| Salon, gym or studio | Instagram, WhatsApp, Walk-in, Google | Goal, budget, schedule | Plans, services, trainers | Trial session or tour | Front desk | Sales desk: membership offer and payment | Member Joined |
| Interior design and home services | Website, Instagram, Referral, Ads | Work and style, budget, home size, timeline | Packages and designers | Site visit and measurement | Designer | Designer: quote and contract | Advance Paid |
| Coaching institute | Website, WhatsApp, Walk-in, Ads | Course, batch, budget, goals | Courses and batches | Counselling or demo class | Counsellor | Counsellor: admission offer and fees | Enrolled |
| Other business | Phone, WhatsApp, Website, Instagram… | What they need, budget, details | Your services | Named from your business type (order, appointment, booking, visit) | Team | Team: final quote and confirmation | Closed Won |

Matching outside real estate: first by the words of what the customer needs against the catalogue names, then by budget (up to 10% over). A customer who only wants a slot can be booked straight from the Pipeline row with the **Book …** button.

## 4. WhatsApp (free, by QR code)

1. Settings › Integrations › **Connect with QR (free)**, or type **"connect my WhatsApp"** to Chief.
2. On the business phone: WhatsApp › Settings › Linked devices › Link a device › scan the code.
3. Customer messages land in Front Desk › Enquiries. New numbers: **Accept (add as lead)** or **Decline (never ask again)**.
4. Replies and reminders go out from your number only after you approve them: one person at a time, at most 20 an hour and 100 a day, never groups.
5. Bulk campaigns need the paid official API (Settings › Integrations › Bulk marketing).

## 5. Social media

- Click a festival on the calendar, tick the networks, press **Draft posts**. One version per ticked network waits in Approvals.
- **Facebook**: only Pages can be posted to (Meta's rule), never a personal profile.
- **Instagram**: needs a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page, and a JPEG picture.
- **LinkedIn**: a picture is uploaded first, then attached to the post; it is never pasted as a link.
- If your office network blocks facebook.com ("This website is blocked by your organization"), connect from a network that allows it. The app cannot change that.

## 6. Chief

- Ask plain questions and get instant answers from your records, with no AI cost: "Which invoices are unpaid?", "What is on today?", "Any open enquiries?", "Who needs a follow-up?", "Revenue this month?"
- Ask it to do things ("Draft a Diwali post", "Write a reminder for Ravi"): it drafts, you approve.
- Chief never says "published" unless it can read the post back, and it shows the provider's real error when something fails.

## 7. Usage and privacy

- **Settings › Usage**: AI calls, tokens and cost counted for this account, and for an OpenRouter key the spend OpenRouter itself reports.
- **Settings › Privacy & data**: import a CSV, export everything, delete your data, delete your account.

## 8. For whoever runs the server

- Run `npm install` on the server itself. Never copy `node_modules` from another computer (it breaks picture posts).
- Keep the `data/` folder when you redeploy, or set `DATABASE_URL`. Replacing `data/` removes every account: sign-up says one thing and sign-in another.
